What is Quoridor?

Quoridor is an abstract strategy game for 2 players on a 9×9 board. Each player has one pawn and ten walls. The goal: be the first to reach any square on the opposite side of the board. On your turn you either move your pawn or place a wall — but you can never block the path completely, so winning takes planning, not luck.

How to play in 30 seconds

  1. Your pawn starts at the bottom, your goal is the opposite edge.
  2. On each turn: move the pawn one square, or place one wall (you have 10).
  3. You can jump over your opponent’s pawn if the square behind is free.
  4. You cannot completely block the path to the goal — leave a way through.
  5. First player to reach the opposite side wins.
